How to read the numbers

The scores are warning-signal strength, not the chance of a crash. A 78 does not mean a 78% probability. It means ASRO is seeing relatively strong evidence for that condition compared with its measurement scale.

0–39 · greenLower observed systemic pressure.
40–59 · yellowPressure is building and deserves monitoring.
60–84 · orangeElevated interconnected pressure needs closer attention.
85–100 · redHigh convergence across the measured warning conditions.

The homepage color follows these fixed bands and changes gradually within each band. Red is reserved for scores of 85 or higher.

“Confirmed” in Data status counts reviewed economic events across the whole observatory. Each measurement uses only the confirmed events relevant to that category, so one category can still have no usable evidence.

“Evidence points” tells you how much measured material sits behind a category. A high score with only a few points is less established than the same score supported by hundreds.

The large system score combines the nine categories while requiring evidence from different parts of the hypothesis. “Trend not established” means ASRO does not yet have enough history to say whether pressure is rising or falling.

Counter-evidence gap works in reverse: a high number means ASRO is missing reassuring evidence such as strong cash flow or deleveraging. It does not mean ASRO proved that reassuring evidence does not exist.
