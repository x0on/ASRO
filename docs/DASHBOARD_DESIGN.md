# Dashboard design contract

The homepage answers one question:

> Is the evidence moving the hypothesis closer to, or farther from, the failure scenario?

## Primary object: the living nebula

- nodes = companies, funds, lenders, projects, indexes, institutions
- edges = extracted financial relationships
- density = interdependence / concentration
- temperature = convergence toward the monitored scenario
- motion = relationship activity

The nebula must also cool/disperse when the thesis weakens.

## No fake probability

The convergence index is not a probability of collapse.

## Timeline

The timeline lives on the homepage. Selecting a marker reveals the date,
relationship, amount, evidence sentence, and source.

New markers are visibly **provisional** until the daily evidence review confirms, merges, or
flags them. Provisional activity is intentionally public because it shows the observatory
working in real time; it must never be presented as settled fact.

## Thesis line

The thesis sits above the nebula and expands on click.

## Product hierarchy

thesis → living nebula → trend status → timeline → selected evidence

Trend status says "not yet measured" until historical comparison is implemented.

Deeper forensic pages can contain the full money graph, entities, raw evidence,
methodology, source health, and component scores.
