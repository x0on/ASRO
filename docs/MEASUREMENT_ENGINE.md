# ASRO Measurement Engine

ASRO now separates source documents, extracted financial events, and normalized observations.

A headline does not directly move the nebula. An extracted event must first map to a defined Data Dictionary variable.

## Wired variables

- AI capital commitments
- external AI revenue
- vendor financing
- AI-related debt
- refinancing stress
- public index exposure
- retirement exposure
- model price pressure
- external capability pressure
- external price-performance pressure
- free cash flow strength
- deleveraging

## Missing data

Missing data remains unknown. It never silently becomes zero.

## High-warning gate

ASRO should not enter its strongest warning state on valuation or news volume alone.
High convergence requires multiple independent dimensions and material evidence in fragility,
transmission, and stress.

## External competitive pressure

External pressure is source-neutral. It can come from Chinese frontier models, open-weight
ecosystems, European models, architectural breakthroughs, or new hardware/software stacks.

The measured question is:

> Is an external competitor materially reducing the price, scarcity, or expected economic life
> of capital already committed by incumbents?
